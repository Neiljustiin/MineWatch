HOW DETECTION WORKS · 04 STAGES · AI MODEL + HUMAN REVIEW

Four stages. The last one is always a person.

01 · SATELLITE PASS · 10 m

Sentinel-2 imagery, every pass

Sentinel-2 L2A surface reflectance at 10 m, roughly a 5-day revisit. Cloud-masked scenes are paired per area of interest: before (T0) and after (T1).

OUT → scene pair T0 / T1
02 · AI CHANGE DETECTION

FC-Siam-diff v4 flags what changed

A trained Siamese change-detection network (FC-Siam-diff v4) compares T0 and T1 and outputs disturbance polygons, each with a confidence score.

OUT → polygon, area (ha), confidence
03 · PERMIT CROSS-CHECK

Each polygon meets the permit map

Polygons are intersected with BIG Kebijakan Satu Peta mining-permit polygons and given one of three statuses:

REDNo matching permit
YELLOWPermit exists, expired
GREENInside a valid permit
04 · ANALYST REVIEW & SIGN-OFF

A named analyst decides

The analyst reviews imagery, NDVI change and permit overlap, then records a signed decision. Nothing is sent to DLHK or Polda before that.

Confirmed illegal Needs field verification False positive Legal
OUT → signed decision, then alert

LIMITATIONS · READ BEFORE RELYING ON ANY FLAG

Current prototype status, stated plainly
DETECTION · AI MODEL

Detection uses a trained AI change-detection model (FC-Siam-diff v4), not a human visually comparing images — but it is still a flag, not a legal verdict.

PERMIT DATA

Permit polygons are a ~2022 BIG snapshot, not real-time. Permits issued, renewed or revoked since then are not reflected.

REVIEW

Every flagged case requires analyst sign-off. A flag is a lead to examine, never an automatic verdict.

ALERTS · MOCK

Alert dispatch to DLHK / Polda is currently a mock, not a live integration. No message leaves the system.
